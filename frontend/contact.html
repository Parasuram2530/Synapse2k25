Frequently Asked Questions

How do I create an account?

Click on the "Get Started" button on our homepage and fill out the registration form. You'll receive a confirmation email to activate your account.

Is StudyZone free to use?

Yes! StudyZone offers a free tier with all essential features. We also offer premium plans with advanced features for institutions.

How do I reset my password?

Use the "Forgot Password" link on the login page. We'll send you a secure link to reset your password.

Can I access StudyZone on mobile devices?

Absolutely! StudyZone is fully responsive and works seamlessly on all devices including smartphones and tablets.

How do I upload course materials?

As a teacher, navigate to your course dashboard and use the "Upload Material" feature. We support various file formats including PDF, images, and videos.

Is my data secure?

Yes, we use industry-standard encryption and security measures to protect your data. Your privacy and security are our top priorities.
